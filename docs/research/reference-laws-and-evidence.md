Interpretation for the demo: Estonia's once-only rule is not a customer-service slogan; it is a statutory design
constraint on *databases* (§ 43³(2) forbids building a second register for the same data; § 43⁶ forces other
registers to consume the "basic data" of the authoritative one), backed by a mandatory exchange layer that logs
every query (§ 43⁹(3) + Regulation 105/2016 § 2 cl. 16). That is exactly the topology rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Legal basis for Singpass being accepted everywhere.** No single statute obliges agencies or businesses to
accept Singpass. Government-wide acceptance rests on (a) the PSGA's directed data sharing for
government-to-government flows, (b) whole-of-government policy decisions (DGB KPI 4: 100% pre-fill; Digital IC
circular of Nov 2021), and (c) the ETA for legal effect of electronic signatures. Private-sector acceptance is
voluntary and market-driven. This is a governance-by-mandate-of-the-executive model, not a legislated
acceptance duty — a real difference from eIDAS 2.0 (below) and from Costa Rica's TSE resolution.

### Were verified savings ever tied by law to a fund or to tax relief?

Short answer: **no clean precedent was found.** The closest cases are budget-side "harvesting" of expected savings,
and audits show even that is hard.

| Case | What happened | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Denmark — mandatory Digital Post** (businesses 1 Nov 2013, citizens 1 Nov 2014) and mandatory digital self-service on 89 service areas | Agency for Digitisation estimated ~DKK 1 bn/yr savings; savings were taken through the annual finance agreements: "Block grants have been reduced by almost half the agreed savings amount, whereas the other half… will be used by the municipalities and regions for welfare purposes." Central government appropriations cut by only DKK 48 M in 2015 vs DKK 244 M potential; SKAT and the Police "found it necessary to find savings in other areas". Rigsrevisionen: "unlikely that the transition to Digital Post will generate annual savings of approximately DKK 1 billion." | Savings pre-booked in budgets (a "digitisation dividend" by negotiation, not by statute); partly reinvested in welfare by agreement; audited as over-estimated. [Rigsrevisionen report 9/2015 (PDF)](https://www.uk.rigsrevisionen.dk/Media/7/1/9-2015.pdf); [digst.dk background](https://en.digst.dk/systems/digital-post/about-the-national-digital-post/background/) |
| **United Kingdom — GDS / "digital by default"** | Cabinet Office claimed £1.7 bn saved in 2014-15 through digital and technology transformation (£391 M spend controls, £103.8 M PSN, £61.5 M GOV.UK, ~£1.1 bn departmental). NAO (2017): spend controls reduced IT spending by £1.3 bn over five years, but the evidence for savings was "hard to follow"/"poorly evidenced". Savings went to the Treasury's efficiency targets, not to an earmarked fund or tax relief. | Efficiency accounting, contested; no legal earmark. [GDS blog 2015](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2015/10/23/how-digital-and-technology-transformation-saved-1-7bn-last-year); [NAO 2017 report](https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/digital-transformation-in-government/) |
| **New Zealand — Better Public Services Result 10** | Target: 70% of the most common transactions digital by 2017 (raised to 80% by 2021). Leadership stated "it isn't about saving money but rather about enhancing service delivery." | Service target, not a savings mechanism. [DIA Result 10 (archived)](https://www.dia.govt.nz/Better-Public-Services) |
| **Estonia** | No statutory link between X-Road savings and any fund; the 2% figure is an expectation, not an accounted saving (see above). | None. |
| **EU** | The €5 bn once-only estimate and e-procurement savings are ex-ante models; no EU instrument earmarks them. | None. |

A "digital dividend" clause for Costa Rica (e.g. a share of *verified* savings flowing to a named fund or to
debt reduction under Ley 9635's rule) would therefore be novel. The honest framing for the demo is: the reference
countries harvested savings through ordinary budget negotiations, their auditors found the harvest smaller and
lumpier than estimated, and none legislated the link.
